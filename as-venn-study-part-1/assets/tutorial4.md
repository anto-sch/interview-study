##### Solving Tasks with Visual Cues

There are tasks for which the context describes incomplete or incorrect arguments consisting of one or more premises and a conclusion. The question asks about required assumptions or flaws in the reasoning. In this case, the Venn diagrams will first show you only the premises of the argument and then the premises combined with the conclusion. This way you can compare the two, to see which assumptions are required or where errors have been made. Here is an example:
